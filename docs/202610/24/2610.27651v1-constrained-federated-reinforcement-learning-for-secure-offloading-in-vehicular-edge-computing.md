## 摘要
车载边缘计算（VEC）通过将任务卸载至路侧边缘服务器，降低计算密集型车载应用的时延。然而，真实车载工作负载由具有严格可靠性需求的依赖子任务构成，而用于自适应调整卸载策略的跨区域协同学习必须对异构数据保持鲁棒，并在开放无线信道上保障安全。我们提出 CRAFT，一种基于约束强化学习的自适应联邦任务卸载框架，将有向无环图（DAG）结构下的卸载与资源分配建模为约束马尔可夫决策过程。CRAFT 将集中式评论家策略学习与拉格朗日松弛相结合，以显式调控长期任务完成约束，并采用带近端正则化的两层联邦聚合，以协同缓解非独立同分布（non-IID）漂移并稳定约束自适应。联邦参数交换的机密性与完整性由后量子同态加密和基于格的签名提供，且摊销开销适中。在基于虚拟机、覆盖真实墨尔本中央商务区网络并采用真实计算内核的测试平台上，CRAFT 相比最先进基线将加权成本最多降低 42%，保持约 96% 的完成率，并增加 6.4% 的安全开销。

## Abstract
Vehicular Edge Computing (VEC) reduces latency for computation-intensive vehicular applications by offloading tasks to roadside edge servers. Real vehicular workloads, however, consist of dependent subtasks with stringent reliability needs, while the cross-region collaborative learning used to adapt offloading policies must stay robust to heterogeneous data and secure over open wireless channels. We propose CRAFT, a Constrained Reinforcement-based Adaptive Federated Task Offloading framework that casts Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG)-structured offloading and resource allocation as a Constrained Markov Decision Process. CRAFT couples centralized-critic policy learning with Lagrangian relaxation to explicitly regulate the long-term task-completion constraint, and uses two-tier federated aggregation with proximal regularization to jointly mitigate non-Independent and Identically Distributed (non-IID) drift and stabilize constraint adaptation. Confidentiality and integrity for federated parameter exchange are provided by post-quantum homomorphic encryption and lattice-based signatures with modest amortized overhead. On a Virtual Machine-based testbed over a realistic Melbourne Central Business District network with real computation kernels, CRAFT cuts weighted cost by up to 42% over state-of-the-art baselines, maintains a completion rate of around 96%, and adds 6.4% security overhead.
