## 摘要
我们考虑一个按订单生产的生产系统的动态控制问题，该系统服务于对价格和延迟敏感的顾客。该生产系统被建模为一个多类排队网络，其中顾客的延迟敏感性通过吞吐时间约束来刻画。为了在满足这些吞吐时间约束的同时最大化其利润，系统管理者采用三类不同的控制：i）动态定价决策；ii）动态服务顺序（调度）决策；iii）新订单到达时的外包决策。我们在重交通条件下用多维布朗控制问题对该问题进行近似，并开发了一种面向高维问题的基于神经网络的计算方法。通过将解在原模型背景下进行解释，我们提出了一种集动态定价、外包和调度于一体的策略。通过对文献中两个测试问题的仿真研究，证明了该策略的有效性。

## Abstract
We consider dynamic control of a make-to-order production system that serves price- and delay-sensitive customers. The production system is modeled as a multiclass queueing network where the customers' delay sensitivity is modeled via throughput-time constraints. To maximize her profit while meeting these throughput time constraints, the system manager uses three different types of control: i) dynamic pricing decisions; ii) dynamic order-of-service (scheduling) decisions; and iii) outsourcing decisions upon arrival of new orders. We approximate this problem by a multi-dimensional Brownian control problem in heavy traffic, and develop a neural network-based computational method tailored for high-dimensional problems. By interpreting the solution in the context of the original model, we propose an integrated dynamic pricing, outsourcing, and scheduling policy. The effectiveness of this policy is demonstrated through a simulation study of two test problems drawn from the literature.
