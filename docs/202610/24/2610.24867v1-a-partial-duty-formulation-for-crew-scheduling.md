## 摘要
我们考虑一个带有休息、工作时间和值勤长度约束的乘务排班问题。通常采用下潜启发式求解乘务排班问题，并使用列生成求解集合划分模型的 LP 松弛，其中变量对应于值勤。我们证明计算该模型的 LP 界是弱 NP 难的，并提出一种基于部分值勤的替代模型，其 LP 界可在多项式时间内计算。我们证明相应的定价问题具有相同的复杂度结果。然而，部分值勤模型的 LP 界较弱。为克服该问题，我们引入相容性割，其完全弥补了 LP 界的损失。尽管分离可以高效完成，我们证明分离后相应的定价问题是弱 NP 难的。因此，我们还引入相容性割的两个松弛版本。我们通过基于部分值勤模型的下潜启发式的数值实验，在荷兰鹿特丹地区地铁网络的案例研究中展示了计算优势。

## Abstract
We consider a crew scheduling problem with a break, working time, and duty length constraint. It is common to solve crew scheduling problems with a diving heuristic, using column generation to solve the LP relaxation of a set partitioning formulation where variables correspond with duties. We prove that computing the LP bound of this formulation is weakly NP-hard, and propose an alternative formulation based on partial duties, whose LP bound can be computed in polynomial time. We prove the same complexity results for the corresponding pricing problems. However, the partial duty formulation has a weaker LP bound. To overcome this issue, we introduce compatibility cuts which completely recover the loss in LP bound. While separation can be done efficiently, we prove that the corresponding pricing problem after separation is weakly NP-hard. Therefore, we also introduce two relaxed versions of the compatibility cuts. We demonstrate computational benefits by providing numerical experiments with a diving heuristic based on the partial duty formulation, in a case study on the metro network of the Rotterdam area, the Netherlands.
