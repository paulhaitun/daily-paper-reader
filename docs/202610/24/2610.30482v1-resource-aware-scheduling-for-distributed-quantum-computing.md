## 摘要
分布式量子计算（DQC）是一种有前景的近期方法，用于执行超出单个量子处理单元（QPU）能力的电路。已有工作在电路划分与重构方面取得了显著进展，但实际的DQC需要的不仅仅是将电路切割成更小的片段。运行时系统必须决定是完整执行电路、将其切割后在单个QPU上顺序执行，还是将其分布到多个QPU上执行，同时考虑排队、通信、重构和硬件约束。我们提出了一种面向DQC的资源感知调度器，它在运行时使用QPU执行、通信和主机侧重构的显式模型，以及从模拟IBM QPU获得的硬件信息驱动的时序，来比较这些执行方案。通过将通信和重构视为一等调度成本，该调度器使用比仅切割方法更现实的端到端开销视角来选择执行计划。在我们的实验中，重构占切割开销的83%以上，相对于未切割的单QPU执行，分布式执行将P90延迟最多降低39%，而调度开销本身可忽略不计，从未超过端到端作业延迟的0.038%。这些结果共同表明，必须将DQC视为同时涉及量子资源和经典资源的运行时调度问题，而不仅仅是电路划分。

## Abstract
Distributed quantum computing (DQC) is a promising near-term approach for executing circuits that exceed the capacity of a single quantum processing unit (QPU). Prior work has made significant progress on circuit partitioning and reconstruction, but practical DQC requires more than cutting a circuit into smaller pieces. A runtime system must determine whether to execute the circuit intact, cut it for sequential execution on one QPU, or distribute it across multiple QPUs, while accounting for queueing, communication, reconstruction, and hardware constraints. We present a resource-aware scheduler for DQC that compares these execution alternatives at runtime using explicit models of QPU execution, communication, and host-side reconstruction, together with hardware-informed timing derived from simulated IBM QPUs. By treating communication and reconstruction as first-class scheduling costs, the scheduler selects execution plans using a more realistic view of end-to-end overhead than cutting-only approaches. Across our experiments, reconstruction accounts for over 83% of cutting overhead, distributed execution reduces P90 latency by up to 39% relative to uncut single-QPU execution, and scheduling overhead itself remains negligible, never exceeding 0.038% of end-to-end job latency. Together, these results show that DQC must be treated as a runtime scheduling problem over both quantum and classical resources, not just circuit partitioning.
