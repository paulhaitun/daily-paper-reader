## 摘要
章鱼凭借高度可适应的软体展现出一种强大的具身智能形式。其中一个关键机制是能够在臂部任意位置形成弯曲点，从而实现高性能且灵巧的行为。受章鱼灵巧性的启发，本研究聚焦于通过自适应改变沿臂的锚定端点来改变腱驱动臂的曲率。臂内集成了一组可充气外壳；使某个外壳充气会增加该位置的摩擦，卡阻腱并形成一个临时的新锚定点。这种新型软体驱动器不会破坏驱动段与松弛段之间臂的连续性。实验表明，驱动段具有非恒定的钟形曲率，与真实章鱼臂在伸取运动中的曲率相当。与传统单腱连续体机械臂相比，所提出的设计能够通过选择性曲率调节独立塑造臂的不同区域，从而提供更大的工作空间和更高的灵巧性。同时，它避免了通常与多段、多驱动器系统相关的机械复杂性。

## Abstract
Octopuses exhibit a powerful form of embodied intelligence from a highly adaptable soft body. One key mechanism is the ability to instantiate bending points at any location along the arm, which enables performant and dexterous behaviours. Taking inspiration from the octopus for its dexterity, this study focuses on changing the curvature of a tendon-actuated arm by adaptively changing the anchoring end-point along the arm. An array of inflatable shells is integrated into the arm, where inflating a shell causes an increase in friction at the location, jamming the tendon and creating a temporary new anchoring point. The novel soft actuator does not break the continuity of the arm between the actuated segment and the loose segment. Experiments show that the actuated segment has a non-constant bell-shaped curvature which is comparable to that of a real octopus arm during a reaching motion. Compared to conventional single-tendon continuum manipulators, the proposed design offers an expanded workspace and enhanced dexterity through the ability to independently shape different regions of the arm through selective curvature tuning. At the same time, it avoids the mechanical complexity typically associated with multi-segment, multi-actuator systems.
