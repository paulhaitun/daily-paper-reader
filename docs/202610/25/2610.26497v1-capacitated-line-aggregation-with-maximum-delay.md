## 摘要
我们研究具有每批最大延迟的容量约束线聚合问题。请求随时间到达一条有根半直线上的位置，并以至多 $k$ 个请求的瞬时批次被服务。每个批次支付其最远请求的距离加上其最旧请求的等待时间。目标是最小化总成本。

我们给出一个确定性在线算法，竞争比为 $36+12\sqrt3$。该算法沿嵌套时间区间的路径使用 Next Fit 思想：当路径负载测试允许时，将新组分配给现有批次，否则开启新批次。路径负载同时控制容量和开启新批次的成本。对公共尺度进行随机化后，可将针对 oblivious adversary 的期望竞争比改进为 $(18+6\sqrt3)/\ln2$。这两个保证均可扩展到任意不可分割的请求大小。

我们还研究学习增强模型中的容量约束 LAD。在其服务时刻揭示的建议允许相对于每个完整可行建议调度达到 $(1+ε)$-一致性，并具有 $O(ε^{-2})$ 鲁棒性。分析将重复切换的代价计入剩余建议成本的连续下降。

## Abstract
We study capacitated line aggregation with per-batch maximum delay. Requests arrive over time at locations on a rooted half-line and are served in instantaneous batches of at most $k$ requests. Each batch pays the distance to its farthest request plus the waiting time of its oldest request. The objective is to minimize the total cost.   We give a deterministic online algorithm with competitive ratio $36+12\sqrt3$. The algorithm uses a Next Fit idea along paths of nested time intervals: it assigns a new group to an existing batch when a path-load test permits, and opens a new batch otherwise. The path load controls both capacity and the cost of opening new batches. Randomizing the common scale improves the expected competitive ratio to $(18+6\sqrt3)/\ln2$ against an oblivious adversary. Both guarantees extend to arbitrary indivisible request sizes.   We also study capacitated LAD in the learning-augmented model. Advice revealed at its service times allows $(1+ε)$-consistency against every complete feasible advice schedule and $O(ε^{-2})$-robustness. The analysis charges repeated switches to successive decreases in the remaining advice cost.
