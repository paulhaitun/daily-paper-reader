## 摘要
集群准入网关可能收到不再匹配当前可调度性的容量记录。我们研究在准入与不可用联合概率受约束条件下的一个外生二元可调度性单元。对于对称马尔可夫状态，我们推导固定观测延迟下的平稳准入前沿，并对带延迟标签的已送达记录应用有限似然分配。单侧精确二项端点校准每个拟合策略，精确枚举在每个测试参数单元中确认逐策略置信目标。一个独立的确定性防护将每次未决准入计入已实现配额，并在真实结果反馈下在每个前缀处限制不可用状态准入。一个可执行准入过程组合该分配与防护。受控系统将源采样、NATS 投递、随机化准入、Kubernetes 对象和 API 派生结果绑定到单一仅追加记录中。独立审计未在已完成功能块中发现缺失事件、身份不匹配或状态不匹配。一个单独的 CPU 请求核算检查在从不可调度到完成的转换中保持 Job 和 Pod 身份。

## Abstract
A cluster admission gate can receive a capacity record that no longer matches current schedulability. We study one exogenous binary schedulability unit under a bound on the joint probability of admission and unavailability. For a symmetric Markov state, we derive the stationary admission frontier for a fixed observation delay and apply finite likelihood allocation to a delay-tagged delivered record. A one-sided exact binomial endpoint calibrates each fitted policy, and exact enumeration confirms the per-policy confidence target in every tested parameter cell. A separate deterministic guard charges every unresolved admission against the realized allowance and bounds unavailable-state admissions at every prefix under truthful outcome feedback. An executable admission procedure composes the allocation and guard. The controlled system binds source sampling, NATS delivery, randomized admission, Kubernetes objects, and API-derived outcomes in one append-only record. Independent audit finds no missing event, identity mismatch, or state mismatch in the completed functional block. A separate CPU request-accounting check preserves Job and Pod identity across an unschedulable-to-complete transition.
