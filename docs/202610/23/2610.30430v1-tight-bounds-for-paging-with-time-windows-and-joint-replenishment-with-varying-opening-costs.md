## 摘要
我们研究带时间窗的分页问题，它是经典分页问题的一种自然推广，其中每个请求必须在指定的时间区间内得到服务。该模型允许算法延迟服务并随时间批量处理请求，但由于请求重叠也带来了显著挑战。虽然该问题在无权情形下已被充分理解，已知最优的 O(log k)-竞争算法，但加权情形仍然更难以解决。此前已知最好的算法由 Gupta、Kumar 和 Panigrahi 提出，达到 O(log k log n) 的竞争比，这在加权与无权情形之间留下了差距。我们为带时间窗的加权分页得到一个 O(log k)-竞争算法，匹配最优竞争比。我们方法的核心是一种结构分解，将缓存的演化与页面加载的调度分离。我们证明，给定一系列缓存配置，计算近乎最优的页面加载序列可归约为具有截止时间的联合补货问题（JRP-D）的一个变体，其中开启成本随时间变化。我们为该变体设计了一个常数竞争比的在线算法，并证明该归约是紧的。将其与已有的缓存配置在线构造相结合，即可得到所需界。

## Abstract
We study the paging with time windows problem, a natural generalization of classical paging, in which each request must be served within a specified time interval. This model allows the algorithm to delay service and batch service requests over time, but also introduces significant challenges due to overlapping requests. While the problem is well understood in the unweighted setting, where optimal $O(\log k)$-competitive algorithms are known, the weighted case remains more elusive. The best previously known algorithm, due to Gupta, Kumar, and Panigrahi, achieves an $O(\log k \log n)$-competitive ratio, leaving a gap between weighted and unweighted settings.   We obtain an $O(\log k)$-competitive algorithm for weighted paging with time windows, matching the optimal competitive ratio. The core of our approach is a structural decomposition that separates the evolution of the cache from the scheduling of page loads. We show that, given a sequence of cache configurations, computing a near-optimal sequence of page loads reduces to a variant of the Joint Replenishment Problem with deadlines (JRP-D) with time-dependent opening costs. We design a constant-competitive online algorithm for this variant and prove that the reduction is tight. Combining this with an existing online construction of cache configurations yields the desired bound.
