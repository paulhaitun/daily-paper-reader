## 摘要
我们研究动态环境下的负载均衡问题。输入是由作业集合 $J$ 与机器集合 $M$ 构成的二部图 $G = (J \cup M, E)$。每个作业具有正权重 $w(j)$。目标是将 $J$ 中的每个作业分配给 $M$ 中一个相邻机器，以最小化最大机器负载，其中一台机器的负载为分配给它的作业权重之和。尽管负载均衡已在许多计算模型中得到广泛研究，动态模型中此前唯一的结果针对的是\emph{增量}情形，其中更新形式为边插入和作业删除 [BKS23b]。作者给出了增量\emph{分数}负载均衡的一个 $(1+ε)$-近似算法，更新时间为 $\text{poly}(\log(n),1/ε)$；他们指出其技术不能推广到减量情形。主要结果：我们得到一个算法，在边删除和作业插入下，以 $\tilde{O}(\log (n), 1/ε)$ 更新时间维护分数负载均衡问题的一个 $(1+ε)$-近似解。我们的技术与 [BKS23b] 的增量算法非常不同。相反，我们的算法受计算 {\sc congest} 模型中的一个负载均衡算法启发 [ALPZ21]。我们为该算法开发了一个新的分析框架，使我们能够证明它在动态环境中表现良好。舍入方案：我们还提出了一个用于动态负载均衡的黑盒舍入方案。代入我们的分数算法，我们得到一个用于 $(2+ε)$-近似\emph{整数}负载均衡的减量算法，更新时间为 $\text{poly}(\log(n),1/ε)$。通过代入 [BKS23b] 的分数算法，我们也得到了增量负载均衡的相同界。

## Abstract
We study the problem of load balancing in the dynamic setting. The input is a bipartite graph $G = (J \cup M, E)$ of jobs $J$ and machines $M$. Every job has a positive weight $w(j)$. The goal is to assign every job in $J$ to an adjacent machine in $M$ so as to minimize the maximum machine load, where the load of a machine is the sum of the weights of the jobs assigned to it.   Although load balancing has been studied extensively in many models of computation, the only previous result in the dynamic model addresses the \emph{incremental} setting, where updates take the form of edge insertions and job deletions [BKS23b]. The authors show a $(1+ε)$-approximation for incremental \emph{fractional} load balancing with $\text{poly}(\log(n),1/ε)$ update time; they point out that their techniques do not extend to the decremental setting.   Main Result: We obtain an algorithm which maintains a $(1+ε)$-approximate solution to the fractional load balancing problem under edge deletions and job insertions in $\tilde{O}(\log (n), 1/ε)$ update time. Our techniques are very different from those of the incremental algorithm of [BKS23b]. Instead, our algorithm is inspired by a load-balancing algorithm from the {\sc congest} model of computation [ALPZ21]. We develop a novel analytic framework for this algorithm, which allows us to show that it performs well in the dynamic setting.   Rounding Scheme: We also present a black-box rounding scheme for dynamic load balancing. Plugging in our fractional algorithm, we obtain a decremental algorithm for $(2+ε)$-approximate \emph{integral} load balancing with $\text{poly}(\log(n),1/ε)$ update time. We also obtain the same bounds for incremental load balancing by plugging in the fractional algorithm of [BKS23b].
