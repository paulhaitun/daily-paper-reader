## 摘要
HPC 设施收集了多年的作业调度器记录，然而由于缺乏可将其转化为可操作洞察的易用工具，其中大部分运行数据仍未得到充分利用。我们提出 CQTwin，一个用于 HPC 调度数据运行分析的开源数字孪生工具包。CQTwin 通过两种互补模式弥补这一空白：离线轨迹回放和在线事件驱动仿真。CQTwin 构建于基于 Python 的 CQSim 离散事件仿真器之上，将经过保真度验证的高性能 C++ 引擎与交互式浏览器仪表板及 Snapshot Lab 相结合，使设施人员、运维人员和研究人员能够刻画工作负载、比较调度策略并探索假设情景。作为离线孪生，CQTwin 在替代策略下回放归档的工作负载轨迹，以展示同一工作负载在不同调度决策下会如何演变。作为在线孪生，它实时消费到达的作业事件，并预测替代策略将如何处理当前排队的作业，为实时决策提供指导。该仪表板支持完整分析工作流，包括工作负载刻画、实验配置与监控，以及仿真结果的多维比较，使用户能够直观判断哪些策略最适合某一工作负载及其带来的权衡。我们使用阿贡领导力计算设施（Argonne Leadership Computing Facility）2016—2023 年的生产轨迹验证了 CQTwin。

## Abstract
HPC facilities collect years of job scheduler records, yet much of this operational data remains underused because accessible tools for turning it into actionable insight are scarce. We present CQTwin, an open-source digital-twin toolkit for operational analytics of HPC scheduling data. CQTwin addresses this gap through two complementary modes: offline trace replay and online event-driven simulation. Built on the Python-based CQSim discrete-event simulator, CQTwin combines a fidelity-validated, high-performance C++ engine with an interactive browser-based dashboard and a Snapshot Lab, enabling facility staff, operators, and researchers to characterize workloads, compare scheduling policies, and explore what-if scenarios. As an offline twin, CQTwin replays archived workload traces under alternative policies to show how the same workload would have unfolded under different scheduling decisions. As an online twin, it consumes job events as they arrive and projects how alternative policies would process the currently queued jobs, providing guidance for real-time decision-making. The dashboard supports the complete analysis workflow, including workload characterization, experiment configuration and monitoring, and multidimensional comparison of simulation results, allowing users to determine visually which policies best suit a workload and what trade-offs they entail. We validate CQTwin using production traces from the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility spanning 2016-2023.
