## 摘要
3GPP Release 19 支持存储转发卫星接入，其中馈电链路中断期间积累的流量必须通过有限且受几何限制的窗口转发。静态优先级难以在大规模积压之间进行区分，并且忽略了数据包价值的衰减。我们针对状态更新（以目的端新鲜度衡量）和具有截止时间约束的事件报告，在具有有限星载存储的周期性两跳接触下建立 QoS 可行性问题。对于一般接触日历，我们推导了对顺序敏感的丢失、cohort 容量、交付速率和新鲜度界，包括仅状态场景的精确刻画。对于每周期共享一对接触的单一状态流和单一事件类，有限状态规划给出了任意整数年龄阈值下的精确联合可行性。数值结果量化了负载、容量、截止时间和新鲜度容差之间的权衡。

## Abstract
3GPP Release 19 supports store-and-forward satel- lite access, where traffic accumulated during feeder-link outages must be forwarded through finite, geometry-limited windows. Static priority levels offer little discrimination among large backlogs and ignore the decay in packet value. We formulate QoS feasibility for status updates, measured by destination freshness, and deadline-constrained event reports over periodic two-hop contacts with finite onboard storage. For general calendars, we derive ordering-sensitive miss, cohort-capacity, delivery-rate, and freshness bounds, including an exact status-only characterization. For single status flow and single event class sharing a contact pair per period, a finite-state program gives exact joint feasibility for arbitrary integer age thresholds. Numerical results quantify tradeoffs among load, capacity, deadline, and freshness tolerance.
