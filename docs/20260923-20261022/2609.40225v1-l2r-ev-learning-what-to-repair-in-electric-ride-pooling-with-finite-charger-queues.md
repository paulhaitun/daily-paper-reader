## 摘要
电动合乘出行必须联合管理乘客服务、路线、电池和有限充电桩；局部有用的调车可能降低后续服务。我们提出一个离散事件合乘出行仿真器，包含有序乘客停靠点、上车与乘坐时间约束、电池储备、前往充电桩的行驶，以及有限的先到先服务（FCFS）充电队列。在通用调度器之上，我们加入一个学习得到的修复层，它在车辆之间移动未接载乘客，使用监督评分估计后续影响，并使用两阶段近端策略优化（PPO）策略将计算集中于有希望的移动。每个执行的移动必须满足路线、电池和时间约束，并带来即时的路线成本改善。对于更大规模实例，我们限制候选移动数量，并同时应用多个不冲突的修复。在12个留出的曼哈顿场景中（100个请求、30辆电动汽车、每站一个充电插头），监督修复相比无修复将运营成本降低6.57%（12/12胜出），并服务74.92个请求而非72.75个。两阶段PPO与监督修复的差距保持在1.43%以内，同时精确试验次数减少95.4%；我们的实验可扩展到8,000个请求和2,400辆电动汽车，并在所有三个更大开发设置中实现成本降低。队列感知充电将等待时间减少41.0%；在声明的1.5-kW队列空闲负载下，每服务一个请求的运行电力消耗下降1.73%。

## Abstract
Electric ride-pooling must jointly manage passenger service, routes, batteries, and finite chargers; a locally useful relocation can reduce later service. We introduce a discrete-event ride-pooling simulator with ordered passenger stops, pickup and ride-time constraints, battery reserves, charger travel, and finite first-come, first-served (FCFS) charging queues. On top of a common dispatcher, we add a learned repair layer that moves uncollected passengers between vehicles, uses a supervised score to estimate later effects, and uses a two-stage proximal-policy-optimization (PPO) policy to focus computation on promising moves. Every executed move must satisfy route, battery, and time constraints and provide an immediate route-cost improvement. For larger instances, we limit the number of candidate moves and apply several non-conflicting repairs at once. On 12 held-out Manhattan episodes with 100 requests, 30 EVs, and one plug per station, supervised repair reduces operational cost by 6.57\% versus no repair (12/12 wins) and serves 74.92 rather than 72.75 requests. Two-stage PPO stays within 1.43\% of supervised repair with 95.4\% fewer exact trials, while our experiments scale to 8,000 requests and 2,400 EVs with cost reductions across all three larger development settings. Queue-aware charging reduces waiting by 41.0\%; under a declared 1.5-kW queue-idle load, operational electricity per served request falls 1.73\%.
