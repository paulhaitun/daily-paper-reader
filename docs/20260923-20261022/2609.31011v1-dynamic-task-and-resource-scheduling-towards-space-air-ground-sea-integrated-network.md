## 摘要
在6G泛在连接背景下，空天地海一体化网络（SAGSIN）成为泛在服务供给的新范式。为支持基础设施匮乏海域中日益扩展的海上活动，我们提出一种面向SAGSIN的创新性动态任务与资源调度方法，为船舶提供计算服务。该方法集成广覆盖卫星、具备中继能力的高空平台（HAP）、能量充足的沿海基站（BS）以及灵活部署的无人机（UAV），以支撑广域、高机动和持续的海上服务。为解决跨四层任务调度难题，设计了动态任务卸载算法。该算法基于实时系统状态，将任务流导向负载较轻、计算能力强且链路速率高的服务器，以降低任务执行时延，并融合预判式卫星切换策略，以缓解切换后拥塞并提高卫星资源利用率。考虑无人机续航能力有限，我们引入剩余能量约束，以确保任务积压交接和安全返航。此外，联合优化无人机-基站带宽分配、无人机轨迹和计算资源分配，以增强低空设备间的连通性并加速任务完成。仿真结果验证了所提方法在复杂海上环境中任务执行期间对系统资源变化的优越适应性，相较于基准方法，平均任务时延至少降低23%。

## Abstract
In the context of 6G ubiquitous connectivity, the space-air-ground-sea integrated network (SAGSIN) emerges as a new paradigm for pervasive service provisioning. To support expanding maritime activities in infrastructure-scarce ocean areas, we propose an innovative dynamic task and resource scheduling approach for SAGSIN to deliver computing services for vessels. It integrates broad-coverage satellites, relay-capable high-altitude platform (HAP), energy-sufficient coastal base station (BS), and flexibly deployed uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) to accommodate wide-area, highly mobile, and sustained maritime services. To address the challenge of task scheduling across four layers, a dynamic task offloading algorithm is developed. It steers task flows toward servers with light loads, strong computing capabilities, and high-rate links based on real-time system states to reduce task execution delay, integrating an anticipatory satellite handover strategy to mitigate post-handover congestion and improving satellite resource utilization. Considering the limited endurance of UAVs, we impose residual energy constraints to ensure task backlog handover and safe return. Furthermore, the UAV-BS bandwidth allocation, UAV trajectories, and computing resource allocation are jointly optimized to enhance the connectivity among low-altitude devices and accelerate task completion. Simulation results validate the proposed method's superior adaptability to system resource variations during task execution in complex maritime environments, achieving at least a 23% reduction in average task delay over benchmarks.
