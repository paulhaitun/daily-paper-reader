## 摘要
电动汽车（EV）和氢燃料电池汽车（HFCV）是减少碳排放努力中具有前景的技术。基础设施有限、消费者不满以及电网压力等挑战阻碍了这些技术的采用进展。一种能够服务EV和HFCV的零排放车辆（ZEV）站点，通过聚焦于最大化站点利润和消费者效用、同时最小化电网影响来应对这些挑战。本研究旨在开发一个ZEV站点的逼真仿真，该仿真能够处理由强化学习（RL）实现的实时定价决策，并将RL智能体的性能与静态定价方法进行比较。训练后，RL智能体获得的站点利润比基线人工参数搜索高10.83%。这项工作为ZEV站点所有者在处理复杂定价和采购决策时提供了一个有用的工具。

## Abstract
Electric vehicles (EVs) and hydrogen fuel cell vehi- cles (HFCVs) are promising technologies in the efforts to reduce carbon emissions. Challenges such as limited infrastructure, con- sumer dissatisfaction, and electrical grid strain inhibit progress in the adoption of these technologies. A zero emission vehicle (ZEV) station, capable of handling EVs and HFCVs, addresses these challenges by focusing on maximizing station profit and consumer utility, while minimizing grid impacts. The purpose of this research is to develop a realistic simulation of a ZEV station that is capable of handling real-time pricing decisions enabled by reinforcement learning (RL) and to compare the performance of the RL agent to static pricing methods. After training, the RL agent gains 10.83% greater station profit than the baseline manual parameter search. This work serves as a useful tool for ZEV station owners when handling complex pricing and purchasing decisions.
