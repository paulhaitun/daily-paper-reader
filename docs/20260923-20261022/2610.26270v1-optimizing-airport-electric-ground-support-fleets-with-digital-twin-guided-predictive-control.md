## 摘要
机场地面作业电气化是降低航空相关排放的关键一步，但这会使任务分配与充电协调在实时层面强耦合。本文构建了面向电动地面保障车辆（EGSVs）的机场地面保障作业数字孪生，将机场作业的基于智能体模型与实时航班时刻更新及车辆能量动态相结合。在该平台上，我们比较了三种EGSV调度与充电控制策略：基于规则的启发式方法、滚动时域模型预测控制器（MPC）和蒙特卡洛树搜索（MCTS）控制器。这些控制器在史基浦机场三个代表性运营日以及多种车队规模下进行了评估。结果表明，延误与能耗之间存在明显权衡。在所有测试场景中，MCTS的平均航班延误最低，汇总延误为4.09分钟，相较启发式和MPC控制器分别改善64.7%和31.5%，但能耗分别增加1%和0.3%。MPC和MCTS控制器在全天更有效地协调充电，并分别将峰值充电基础设施需求减少6.0和4.7个充电单元。此外，我们发现，对于所有控制器，更多车辆可能带来更低的总体能耗和更低的充电基础设施需求。这些发现凸显了数字孪生的价值：它不仅是离线设计评估工具，也是电气化机场运营中实时控制策略的实用环境。

## Abstract
Electrifying airport ground operations is a key step toward reducing aviation-related emissions, but it makes task assignment and charging coordination strongly coupled in real time. This paper develops a digital twin of airport ground handling operations for electric ground-support vehicles (EGSVs), combining an agent-based model of airport operations with live flight-schedule updates and vehicle-energy dynamics. On this platform, we compare three control strategies for EGSV dispatching and charging: a rule-based heuristic, a rolling-horizon model predictive controller (MPC), and a Monte Carlo tree search (MCTS) controller. The controllers are evaluated on three representative Schiphol Airport operating days and for multiple fleet sizes. The results show a clear trade-off between delays and energy use. Across all tested scenarios, MCTS yields the lowest average flight delay, with a pooled delay of 4.09 min, improving on the heuristic and MPC controllers by 64.7\% and 31.5\%, but at an increased energy consumption of 1\% and 0.3\%, respectively.The MPC and MCTS controllers coordinate charging more effectively throughout the day and reduce the peak charging infrastructure required by 6.0 and 4.7 charger units, respectively. Additionally, we find more vehicles can result in lower overall energy consumption and lower charging infrastructure requirements for all controllers. These findings highlight the value of digital twins not only as offline design evaluation tools, but also as practical environments for real-time control policies in electrified airport operations.
