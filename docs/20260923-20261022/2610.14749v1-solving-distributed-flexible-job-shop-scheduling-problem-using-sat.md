## 摘要
分布式柔性作业车间调度问题（DFJSP）需要联合确定工厂分配、机器选择和工序排序，以最小化最大完工时间。本文提出一种基于SAT的精确方法，该方法建立在高效的SAT形式化之上，通过传播、对称性破除和增量式SAT求解来利用问题结构，从而有效处理这些耦合决策。所提方法在二工厂和三工厂配置下，于来自Fattahi、Brandimarte和rdata数据集的76个标准基准实例上进行了评估。结合此前已确立的结果，新的SAT最优性证书使已证明最优的实例总数达到76个中的72个，其中包括三个新认证实例；对于其中两个实例，SAT还改进了此前最佳已知最大完工时间。在各基准配置上，SAT在五种配置中的四种上取得了最佳结果，并与CP和LBBD1_CPIL等强精确方法以及现有元启发式方法保持竞争力。这些结果表明，所提出的SAT框架是DFJSP的一种有效精确方法，兼具强解质量和严格的最优性认证，并为未来DFJSP研究以及在更多基准实例上的评估提供了一种互补的精确方法。

## Abstract
The Distributed Flexible Job Shop Scheduling Problem (DFJSP) requires the joint determination of factory assignment, machine selection, and operation sequencing to minimize the makespan. This paper presents an exact SAT-based approach built on an efficient SAT formulation that exploits problem structure through propagation, symmetry breaking, and incremental SAT solving to effectively address these coupled decisions. The proposed method is evaluated on 76 standard benchmark instances from the Fattahi, Brandimarte, and rdata datasets under two- and three-factory configurations. Together with previously established results, the new SAT optimality certificates bring the total number of proven-optimal instances to 72 out of 76, including three newly certified instances; for two of these instances, SAT also improves the previous best-known makespans. Across the benchmark configurations, SAT achieves the best results on four of the five configurations and remains competitive with strong exact approaches such as CP and LBBD1_CPIL, as well as existing metaheuristic methods. These results demonstrate that the proposed SAT framework is an effective exact approach for the DFJSP, combining strong solution quality with rigorous optimality certification and offering a complementary exact method for future DFJSP studies and evaluations on additional benchmark instances.
