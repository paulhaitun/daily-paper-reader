## 摘要
我们研究向量调度问题，其中每个作业在一个固定时间区间内处于活动状态。一个作业会使用若干种资源，并在其整个区间内驻留在同一台机器上；其资源需求可能取决于机器。目标是最小化所有机器和所有时刻上的最大资源负载。对于 r 台机器和 d 种资源，我们给出一个确定性的 (1+ε)-近似算法，其运行时间为 f(r,d,1/ε)N^{O(1)}，其中 N 是二进制输入长度。对于固定的 r 和 d，这给出了一个高效多项式时间近似方案，扩展了标量临时任务分配问题的近似方案。该算法将作业合并为块，这些块的时间区间在选定任何机器之前就已固定，并通过对时间轴进行平衡递归划分上的动态规划来分配这些块。我们还证明了强 NP-难性，并在指数时间假设下证明了关于 1/ε 的指数下界，即使对于两台相同机器和一种资源也已成立。

## Abstract
We study vector scheduling in which each job is active during a fixed time interval. A job uses several resources and stays on one machine for its entire interval; its resource requirements may depend on the machine. The objective is to minimize the largest resource load over all machines and times. For $r$ machines and $d$ resources, we give a deterministic $(1+\varepsilon)$-approximation in $f(r,d,1/\varepsilon)N^{O(1)}$ time, where $N$ is the binary input length. This gives an efficient polynomial-time approximation scheme for fixed $r$ and $d$, extending approximation schemes for scalar temporary tasks assignment. The algorithm merges jobs into blocks whose time intervals are fixed before any machine is chosen, and assigns the blocks by dynamic programming over a balanced recursive split of the time line. We also prove strong NP-hardness and an exponential lower bound in $1/\varepsilon$ under the Exponential Time Hypothesis, already for two identical machines and one resource.
