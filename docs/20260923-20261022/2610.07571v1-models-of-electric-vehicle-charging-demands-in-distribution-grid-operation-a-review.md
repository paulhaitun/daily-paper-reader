## 摘要
电动汽车（EV）快速融入交通系统，给配电系统（PDS）引入了一类新型负荷，即电动汽车充电需求（EVCD）。EVCD的时空变异性给配电系统运营商（DSO）带来了根本性挑战，尤其是在确保网络安全可靠运行方面。在配电最优潮流（DOPF）问题中将EVCD作为参数准确表征，是实现可靠且经济高效的PDS运行的前提，然而尚未形成普遍接受的建模范式。现有研究涵盖了广泛的假设、数学表述和求解范式。本文从DSO运行视角，系统综述了在DOPF框架中表征EVCD所采用的方法与模型。根据EVCD表征方式，现有EVCD建模方法分为两大类：确定性模型和不确定性感知模型。这两大类又进一步划分为若干子类。本文旨在对将EVCD集成到PDS运行与规划中的模型进行全面综述。

## Abstract
The rapid integration of electric vehicles (EVs) into the transportation system has introduced a new class of load into power distribution systems (PDS), i.e., EV charging demand (EVCD). The spatiotemporal variability of EVCD poses fundamental challenges for distribution system operators (DSOs), particularly in ensuring secure and reliable network operation. Accurately representing EVCD as a parameter within the distribution optimal power flow (DOPF) problem is a prerequisite for reliable, cost-efficient PDS operation, yet no universally accepted modeling framework has emerged. The existing contributions span a wide spectrum of assumptions, mathematical formulations, and solution paradigms. This article presents a systematic review of methods and models employed to represent EVCD within DOPF frameworks from the DSO's operational perspective. The existing EVCD modeling methods are classified into two primary categories based on EVCD representation: deterministic models and uncertainty-aware models. The two main categories are further divided into several subcategories. This paper aims to provide a comprehensive review on models for integrating EVCD into PDS operation and planning.
